About

Curious by default, rigorous by habit.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science at Gautam Buddha University, Greater Noida. My research and development efforts operate at the vanguard of applied AI and quantum computing—I am drawn to complex challenges that lack conventional, textbook solutions. By participating in the McKinsey Forward Program, I honed my ability to navigate ambiguity and execute strategic reasoning. As an IBM Qiskit Advocate, I actively contribute to the open-source quantum ecosystem. Beyond software engineering, I am a published author with a profound fascination for drone technology and the pioneering aerospace and defense initiatives of ISRO, DRDO, and BARC.
